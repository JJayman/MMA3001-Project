treeskel.bend3d

The tree in 3D with bends: smooth curves for the trunk and leaders that may leave the tree's plane.

treeskel.tree3d puts every point of the skeleton on one plane. The trees are flat to a median 1.7 cm, but the depth along a winter trace shows smooth structure beyond that: leaders that bow 5–10 cm towards or away from the camera over tens of centimetres, and trunks that stand behind the leaders' plane. Here each branch may bend out of the plane where the depth says so, and stays on it where the depth says little. For each tree:

  1. Depth to each branch's axis (axis_distance()): the depth reads a branch's near surface, so its axis lies about one radius farther (the radius from the 2D width, at the point's distance). Without this, the trunk (radius about 5 cm) would read about 3 cm nearer than the leaders (about 2 cm): a false step at the fork.
  2. Which readings count (ridge_contrast()): in winter, every reading (the bare tree is in view). In summer, only where the branch is a depth ridge: both sides, RIDGE_GAP px beyond its edge, read at least RIDGE_CONTRAST units (about 5 cm) farther, or nothing. Inside the canopy the reading at a branch pixel is usually a leaf in front, and the depth there is a smooth surface with no ridge; the trunk below the canopy, and wood seen against a gap, pass.
  3. The plane: treeskel.tree3d.fit_plane() through every reading's axis point (the reference the bends are measured from).
  4. The bends (fit_bends()): along each part, the distance to its axis along the pixel's ray is Z = Z_plane + h(s). The bend h is a smooth function of the length s along the branch, with the plane as a preference: a Gaussian-process prior of standard deviation spread (τ) and length length (L), whose precision is (1 + L⁴ d⁴/ds⁴) / (2√2 τ² L). Well-observed sections follow the readings; sections with few or no readings stay near the plane, with a posterior standard deviation (posterior_sd()) that says so. The three parts share the fork's bend, so they stay connected, and each may kink there. Each reading counts as Δs / ℓ independent ones (CORR_PX): neighbouring readings share most of their pixels. The fit is robust: Huber weights first, then Tukey's biweight, so background readings and depth jumps (a post or another branch) get no weight.
  5. The 3D curves (bent_points()): every point moves along its own ray, so the bent skeleton still projects exactly onto the 2D trace.
  6. Is there anything to find? (block_cv()): blocks of BLOCK_PX px along each part are left out in turn (with BUFFER_PX px either side, so correlated noise cannot leak in), and their depth is predicted from the rest, by the plane alone (τ = 0) and by each prior on GRID. Smooth bends predict a left-out block from its neighbours; noise does not.
  7. Measures (bend_measures()), alignment-free so that winter and summer can be compared tree by tree: how far each part leaves the plane; the trunk's angle out of the V's plane (the plane through both leader lines; positive when the trunk's lower end is behind it); each leader's bow out of that plane at the middle of its stretch; the V opening between the bent leaders' lines; the leaders' lengths. With the plane model the trunk's angle and the bows are 0.

Distances are in metres: the depth units times per_unit(), which puts the median development fork at 2.0 m for each camera setting, as treeskel.tree3d.scales() does.

 107def per_unit(camera: Camera) -> float:
 108    """Metres per depth unit for a camera setting: the median development fork at 2.0 m.

 152def axis_distance(values: np.ndarray, radii_px: np.ndarray, camera: Camera) -> np.ndarray:
 153    """Distance (units, along the optical axis) to a branch's axis, from the depth of its near surface.
 154
 155    The axis lies one radius behind the surface; a radius of ``r`` px at distance ``Z`` is ``r Z / f``.
 156
 157    Example:
 158        >>> axis_distance(np.array([100.0]), np.array([10.0]), Camera(zero=300.0, focal=500.0)).tolist()
 159        [204.0]
 160    """
 161    z = camera.distance(values)
 162    return z * (1.0 + np.asarray(radii_px, float) / camera.focal)

 165def ridge_contrast(depth: np.ndarray, valid: np.ndarray, path: np.ndarray, radii_px: np.ndarray,
 166                   gap: float = RIDGE_GAP) -> np.ndarray:
 167    """How much farther than a branch its two sides read (depth units; the smaller of the two sides).
 168
 169    Each side is read ``radius + gap`` px out along the path's normal (median within 2 px); a side with no
 170    reading counts as infinitely far. NaN where the branch itself has no reading.
 171    """
 172    p = np.asarray(path, float).reshape(-1, 2)
 173    normals = path_normals(np.rint(p).astype(int), 6)
 174    centre = depth_along(depth, valid, p)
 175    out = np.full(len(p), np.inf)
 176    for sign in (-1.0, 1.0):
 177        side = p + sign * (np.asarray(radii_px, float) + gap)[:, None] * normals
 178        v = depth_along(depth, valid, side, radius=2, min_valid=3)
 179        out = np.minimum(out, np.where(np.isnan(v), np.inf, centre - v))
 180    out[np.isnan(centre)] = np.nan
 181    return out

 205        s: Per part, the length along the chain on the plane (m), from the fork.

 229def prepare(paths: dict, depth: np.ndarray, valid: np.ndarray, radii: dict, camera: Camera = Camera(),
 230            ridge: bool = False, scale: float | None = None) -> Prepared:
 231    """One tree's skeleton with its depth, plane and chains.
 232
 233    Args:
 234        paths: ``{"trunk", "left", "right"}`` ``(N, 2)`` ``(row, col)`` photo points: the trunk from the root
 235            to the fork, each leader from the fork to its tip.
 236        depth, valid: The photo's depth and where it has readings.
 237        radii: Each part's radius at each point (photo px).
 238        camera: The depth map and camera.
 239        ridge: Count only the readings where the branch is a depth ridge (:func:`ridge_contrast`; summer).
 240        scale: Metres per unit (default :func:`per_unit` of ``camera``).
 241
 242    Raises:
 243        ValueError: If fewer than 20 points have depth (no plane).
 244    """
 245    pu = per_unit(camera) if scale is None else scale
 246    paths = {p: np.asarray(paths[p], float).reshape(-1, 2) for p in PARTS}
 247    fork = paths["trunk"][-1]
 248    # The chains from the fork outwards; a leader's first point is the fork itself when it repeats it.
 249    own = {"trunk": paths["trunk"][::-1][1:]}
 250    for p in ("left", "right"):
 251        own[p] = paths[p][1:] if len(paths[p]) and np.allclose(paths[p][0], fork) else paths[p]
 252    rc = np.concatenate([fork[None], *(own[p] for p in PARTS)])
 253    rad = np.concatenate([np.asarray(radii["trunk"], float)[-1:],
 254                          *(np.asarray(radii[p], float)[::-1][1:] if p == "trunk"
 255                            else np.asarray(radii[p], float)[len(radii[p]) - len(own[p]):] for p in PARTS)])
 256    chains, nodes, k = {}, {}, 1
 257    for p in PARTS:
 258        idx = np.arange(k, k + len(own[p]))
 259        chains[p] = np.r_[0, idx]
 260        k += len(own[p])
 261        if p == "trunk":
 262            nodes[p] = chains[p][::-1]                          # root ... fork
 263        else:
 264            nodes[p] = chains[p] if len(paths[p]) > len(own[p]) else idx
 265    values = depth_along(depth, valid, rc)
 266    z_axis = axis_distance(values, rad, camera) * pu
 267    read = np.isfinite(z_axis)
 268    if read.sum() < 20:
 269        raise ValueError(f"only {int(read.sum())} skeleton points have depth: no plane")
 270    anchor = read.copy()
 271    if ridge:
 272        anchor &= ridge_contrast(depth, valid, rc, rad) >= RIDGE_CONTRAST
 273    rays = camera.rays(rc)
 274    plane = fit_plane(rays[read] * z_axis[read, None])
 275    facing = float(np.degrees(np.arccos(np.clip(-plane["normal"][2], -1, 1))))
 276    if facing > MAX_TURN:
 277        # Rays nearly along the plane would put the skeleton metres away: the fit has failed.
 278        raise ValueError(f"the tree's plane is turned {facing:.0f} degrees from the camera: a failed fit")
 279    z_plane = on_plane(rays, plane)[:, 2]
 280    on = rays * z_plane[:, None]
 281    s, s_px = {}, {}
 282    cell_px = np.zeros(len(rc))
 283    for p in PARTS:
 284        c = chains[p]
 285        s[p] = np.r_[0.0, np.cumsum(np.linalg.norm(np.diff(on[c], axis=0), axis=1))]
 286        steps = np.linalg.norm(np.diff(rc[c], axis=0), axis=1)
 287        s_px[p] = np.r_[0.0, np.cumsum(steps)]
 288        half = np.r_[0.0, steps / 2] + np.r_[steps / 2, 0.0]   # half of each neighbouring step
 289        cell_px[c] += half
 290    return Prepared(paths=paths, nodes=nodes, chains=chains, rays=rays, z_axis=z_axis, anchor=anchor,
 291                    cell_px=cell_px, plane=plane, z_plane=z_plane, s=s, s_px=s_px, per_unit=pu)

 294def prior_terms(prep: Prepared) -> tuple[sparse.csc_matrix, sparse.csc_matrix]:
 295    """The prior's two parts on the chains' nodes: ``(mass, curvature)``.
 296
 297    ``hᵀ mass h ≈ ∫ h² ds`` and ``hᵀ curvature h ≈ ∫ (d²h/ds²)² ds``, summed over the three chains, with
 298    the second differences taken within each chain (each may kink at the fork).
 299    """
 300    n = len(prep.z_axis)
 301    mass = np.zeros(n)
 302    rows, cols, vals = [], [], []
 303    r = 0
 304    for p in PARTS:
 305        c, s = prep.chains[p], prep.s[p]
 306        ds = np.maximum(np.diff(s), 1e-4)
 307        mass[c] += np.r_[0.0, ds / 2] + np.r_[ds / 2, 0.0]
 308        for j in range(1, len(c) - 1):
 309            a, b = ds[j - 1], ds[j]
 310            w = np.sqrt((a + b) / 2)
 311            rows += [r, r, r]
 312            cols += [c[j - 1], c[j], c[j + 1]]
 313            vals += [w * 2 / (a * (a + b)), -w * 2 / (a * b), w * 2 / (b * (a + b))]
 314            r += 1
 315    g = sparse.csr_matrix((vals, (rows, cols)), shape=(r, n))
 316    return sparse.diags(np.maximum(mass, 1e-6)).tocsc(), (g.T @ g).tocsc()

 323def precision(terms: tuple, params: BendParams) -> sparse.csc_matrix:
 324    """The prior's precision matrix for ``spread`` τ and ``length`` L: ``(mass + L⁴ curvature) / (2√2 τ² L)``.
 325
 326    The marginal standard deviation of a long chain under it is τ.
 327    """
 328    mass, curv = terms
 329    c = 1.0 / (2 * np.sqrt(2) * params.spread ** 2 * params.length)
 330    return (c * (mass + params.length ** 4 * curv)).tocsc()

 333def _robust_scale(r: np.ndarray) -> float:
 334    return float(1.4826 * np.median(np.abs(r - np.median(r))))

 337def fit_bends(prep: Prepared, params: BendParams = BendParams(), d: np.ndarray | None = None,
 338              hold: np.ndarray | None = None, terms: tuple | None = None) -> dict:
 339    """The bends ``h`` (m behind the plane, along each ray) at every node, fitted robustly.
 340
 341    Solves ``(Q + W/σ²) h = W d/σ²`` with ``Q`` the prior's :func:`precision`, ``d`` the readings' distance
 342    behind the plane and ``W`` each reading's weight (its robust weight times ``cell_px / corr_px``).
 343    Huber weights with the noise ``σ`` re-estimated each round, then Tukey's biweight with ``σ`` fixed.
 344
 345    Args:
 346        prep: The tree (:func:`prepare`).
 347        params: The prior and the robust fit; ``spread`` 0 gives the plane (``h`` = 0).
 348        d: Readings behind the plane to use instead of ``prep.d`` (e.g. against a refitted plane).
 349        hold: Nodes whose readings are left out.
 350        terms: :func:`prior_terms`, to reuse across fits.
 351
 352    Returns:
 353        ``{"h", "weights", "sigma", "observed", "system"}``: ``system`` is the final ``Q + W/σ²`` (for
 354        :func:`posterior_sd`), None for the plane.
 355    """
 356    d = prep.d if d is None else d
 357    obs = np.isfinite(d) & (True if hold is None else ~hold)
 358    n = len(d)
 359    if params.spread <= 0:
 360        return {"h": np.zeros(n), "weights": obs.astype(float), "sigma": float("nan"), "observed": obs,
 361                "system": None}
 362    q = precision(terms or prior_terms(prep), params)
 363    if obs.sum() == 0:                                  # nothing seen: the prior alone, the plane and its spread
 364        return {"h": np.zeros(n), "weights": np.zeros(n), "sigma": float("nan"), "observed": obs, "system": q}
 365    y = np.where(obs, d, 0.0)
 366    cell = prep.cell_px / params.corr_px
 367    sigma = max(_robust_scale(d[obs]), params.min_noise)
 368    w = obs.astype(float)
 369    h = np.zeros(n)
 370
 371    def solve(weights, s):
 372        """The bends for these readings' weights and noise level, and the system solved."""
 373        a = (q + sparse.diags(cell * weights / s ** 2)).tocsc()
 374        return spsolve(a, cell * weights * y / s ** 2), a
 375
 376    a = None
 377    # Huber: bounded influence, the noise level from the residuals each round.
 378    for _ in range(params.iters):
 379        h_new, a = solve(w, sigma)
 380        r = np.where(obs, d - h_new, 0.0)
 381        sigma = max(_robust_scale(r[obs]), params.min_noise)
 382        w = np.where(obs, np.minimum(1.0, params.huber * sigma / np.maximum(np.abs(r), 1e-12)), 0.0)
 383        done = np.max(np.abs(h_new - h)) < 1e-5
 384        h = h_new
 385        if done:
 386            break
 387    # Tukey's biweight from there, with the noise level fixed: far readings get no weight at all.
 388    for _ in range(params.iters):
 389        r = np.where(obs, d - h, 0.0)
 390        u = r / (params.tukey * sigma)
 391        w = np.where(obs & (np.abs(u) < 1), (1 - u ** 2) ** 2, 0.0)
 392        h_new, a = solve(w, sigma)
 393        done = np.max(np.abs(h_new - h)) < 1e-5
 394        h = h_new
 395        if done:
 396            break
 397    return {"h": h, "weights": w, "sigma": sigma, "observed": obs, "system": a}

 400def posterior_sd(fit: dict) -> np.ndarray:
 401    """The posterior standard deviation of each node's bend (m), with the weights and noise as fitted.
 402
 403    It treats the prior, the noise and the plane as known, so it is a lower bound; 0 for the plane.
 404    """
 405    a = fit["system"]
 406    n = len(fit["h"])
 407    if a is None:
 408        return np.zeros(n)
 409    if n <= 4000:
 410        return np.sqrt(np.clip(np.diag(np.linalg.inv(a.toarray())), 0, None))
 411    lu = splu(a.tocsc())
 412    return np.sqrt(np.clip([lu.solve(np.eye(1, n, i).ravel())[i] for i in range(n)], 0, None))

 435def blocks(prep: Prepared, block_px: float = BLOCK_PX, buffer_px: float = BUFFER_PX) -> list[tuple]:
 436    """The left-out blocks: ``(part, scored, removed)`` node masks.
 437
 438    Each part is cut into blocks of ``block_px`` px from the fork outwards (the fork belongs to the trunk's
 439    first block). A block's readings and those within ``buffer_px`` px of it are removed from the fit; only
 440    the block's own are scored. Near the fork the buffer reaches into the other parts.
 441    """
 442    n = len(prep.z_axis)
 443    out = []
 444    for p in PARTS:
 445        c, s = prep.chains[p], prep.s_px[p]
 446        own = np.ones(len(c), bool) if p == "trunk" else np.r_[False, np.ones(len(c) - 1, bool)]
 447        for k in range(int(s[-1] // block_px) + 1):              # the last block holds the chain's end
 448            lo, hi = k * block_px, (k + 1) * block_px
 449            scored, removed = np.zeros(n, bool), np.zeros(n, bool)
 450            scored[c[own & (s >= lo) & (s < hi)]] = True
 451            removed[c[(s >= lo - buffer_px) & (s < hi + buffer_px)]] = True
 452            if lo - buffer_px < 0:                          # the buffer crosses the fork
 453                for q in PARTS:
 454                    removed[prep.chains[q][prep.s_px[q] < buffer_px - lo]] = True
 455            out.append((p, scored, removed))
 456    return out

 459def block_cv(prep: Prepared, grid: Sequence[tuple[float, float]] = GRID, base: BendParams = BendParams(),
 460             block_px: float = BLOCK_PX, buffer_px: float = BUFFER_PX) -> dict[str, dict]:
 461    """Left-out blocks' depth predicted by the plane and by each prior on ``grid``.
 462
 463    For each block (:func:`blocks`) the plane is fitted again without the removed readings, and the block's
 464    readings are predicted by that plane (``h`` = 0) and by the bends fitted to the rest.
 465
 466    Returns:
 467        ``{setting_key: {part: [sum of |error| (m), sum of error² (m²), readings]}}``, with ``"plane"`` first.
 468    """
 469    terms = prior_terms(prep)
 470    settings = [replace(base, spread=0.0)] + [replace(base, spread=t, length=length) for t, length in grid]
 471    out = {setting_key(s): {p: [0.0, 0.0, 0] for p in PARTS} for s in settings}
 472    read = np.isfinite(prep.z_axis)
 473    for part, scored, removed in blocks(prep, block_px, buffer_px):
 474        target = scored & prep.anchor
 475        keep = read & ~removed
 476        if not target.any() or keep.sum() < 20:
 477            continue
 478        plane = fit_plane(prep.rays[keep] * prep.z_axis[keep, None])
 479        if np.degrees(np.arccos(np.clip(-plane["normal"][2], -1, 1))) > MAX_TURN:
 480            continue
 481        d = np.where(prep.anchor, prep.z_axis - on_plane(prep.rays, plane)[:, 2], np.nan)
 482        for s in settings:
 483            h = fit_bends(prep, s, d=d, hold=removed, terms=terms)["h"]
 484            e = d[target] - h[target]
 485            acc = out[setting_key(s)][part]
 486            acc[0] += float(np.abs(e).sum())
 487            acc[1] += float((e ** 2).sum())
 488            acc[2] += int(len(e))
 489    return out

 496BEND_MEASURES = ("v_opening", "trunk_out", "left_bow", "right_bow", "left_tilt", "right_tilt", "left_tilt_whole",
 497                 "right_tilt_whole", "fork_behind", "trunk_out_max",
 498                 "left_out_max", "right_out_max", "trunk_out_rms", "left_out_rms", "right_out_rms",
 499                 "left_length", "right_length", "trunk_uncertain", "left_uncertain", "right_uncertain",
 500                 "trunk_anchors", "left_anchors", "right_anchors", "sigma")
 501"""Columns of :func:`bend_measures`: degrees; ``*_bow``, ``fork_behind``, ``*_out_*`` and ``sigma`` in cm;
 502lengths in m; ``*_uncertain`` and ``*_anchors`` shares of each part's points. ``*_tilt`` is a leader's line on
 503its stretch out of the tree's plane, ``*_tilt_whole`` the line through all of it in view (degrees, positive:
 504the upper end behind)."""
 505
 506
 507def bend_measures(prep: Prepared, h: np.ndarray, sd: np.ndarray | None = None, params: BendParams = BendParams(),
 508                  zoom: float = 1.0, shape: ShapeParams = ShapeParams(), sigma: float = float("nan")) -> dict:
 509    """One tree's measures of its 3D curves (see the module docstring).
 510
 511    Args:
 512        prep: The tree (:func:`prepare`).
 513        h: The bends (:func:`fit_bends`); zeros for the plane.
 514        sd: Their posterior standard deviations (:func:`posterior_sd`), for the uncertain shares.
 515        params: The prior (its ``spread`` and ``uncertain`` set what is uncertain).
 516        zoom: Photo px per winter px, for the stretches (:data:`treeskel.shape.SUMMER_SCALE` in summer).
 517        shape: The stretches of :mod:`treeskel.shape`: the trunk's top and each leader's line.
 518        sigma: The fitted noise (m), reported in cm.
 519
 520    Returns:
 521        :data:`BEND_MEASURES`, NaN where undefined (a leader or the trunk's top too short).
 522    """
 523    out = dict.fromkeys(BEND_MEASURES, float("nan"))
 524    pts = bent_points(prep, h)
 525    behind = behind_plane(prep, pts)
 526    unit = UNIT_PX * zoom
 527    out["fork_behind"] = float(behind[0] * 100)
 528    out["sigma"] = float(sigma * 100)
 529    for p in PARTS:
 530        k = prep.nodes[p]
 531        out[f"{p}_out_max"] = float(np.max(np.abs(behind[k])) * 100)
 532        out[f"{p}_out_rms"] = float(np.sqrt(np.mean(behind[k] ** 2)) * 100)
 533        out[f"{p}_anchors"] = float(np.mean(prep.anchor[k]))
 534        if sd is not None and params.spread > 0:
 535            out[f"{p}_uncertain"] = float(np.mean(sd[k] > params.uncertain * params.spread))
 536    lines, parts = {}, {}
 537    back = -prep.plane["normal"]                        # away from the camera, out of the tree's plane
 538    for side in ("left", "right"):
 539        k = prep.nodes[side]
 540        out[f"{side}_length"] = float(arc_length(pts[k])[-1]) if len(k) > 1 else float("nan")
 541        if len(k) >= shape.min_points:
 542            out[f"{side}_tilt_whole"] = float(np.degrees(np.arcsin(np.clip(line_direction(pts[k]) @ back, -1, 1))))
 543        m = stretch(prep.paths[side], unit, shape)
 544        if m.sum() >= shape.min_points:
 545            parts[side] = pts[k][m]
 546            lines[side] = line_direction(parts[side])
 547            out[f"{side}_tilt"] = float(np.degrees(np.arcsin(np.clip(lines[side] @ back, -1, 1))))
 548    if len(lines) < 2:
 549        return out
 550    out["v_opening"] = angle(lines["left"], lines["right"])
 551    normal = np.cross(lines["left"], lines["right"])
 552    normal /= np.linalg.norm(normal)
 553    if normal[2] > 0:                                   # face the camera, as the tree's plane
 554        normal = -normal
 555    for side, q in parts.items():
 556        # The bow: the middle of the stretch against the middle of its chord, out of the V's plane.
 557        sq = arc_length(q)
 558        mid = np.array([np.interp(sq[-1] / 2, sq, q[:, j]) for j in range(3)])
 559        out[f"{side}_bow"] = float(-(mid - (q[0] + q[-1]) / 2) @ normal * 100)
 560    trunk = prep.paths["trunk"][::-1]                    # from the fork down
 561    st = arc_length(trunk)
 562    if len(trunk) > 1 and st[-1] >= shape.min_trunk:
 563        top = st <= max(shape.trunk_reach * unit, shape.min_trunk)
 564        if top.sum() >= 3:
 565            down = line_direction(pts[prep.nodes["trunk"][::-1]][top])
 566            out["trunk_out"] = float(np.degrees(np.arcsin(np.clip(-down @ normal, -1, 1))))
 567    return out

 620def measure_tree(job: tuple) -> list[dict]:
 621    """Phase 2 for one tree (top-level for worker processes): the chosen prior's 3D curves and measures.
 622
 623    Winter under every camera setting of :data:`treeskel.tree3d.SETTINGS`, with the plane and the bends,
 624    and the block cross-validation again with :data:`BUFFER_PX` x 3 for the plane and the chosen prior;
 625    winter with ridge readings only (``"winter ridges"``: the trunk where it stands clear of the ground) and
 626    summer (the lined-up hand mask and the U-Net's mask, ridge readings only) under the main setting.
 627
 628    Args:
 629        job: ``(record, clicks, winter radii, hand mask or None, U-Net mask or None, BendParams)``.
 630    """
 631    from treeskel.guided_gt import guided_gt
 632    from treeskel.io import load_tree
 633    from treeskel.shape import SUMMER_SCALE
 634    from treeskel.tree3d import SETTINGS
 635
 636    rec, clicks, radii, hand, unet, params = job
 637    images = load_tree(rec, "winter")
 638    gt = guided_gt(images["rgb"], images["depth"], images["valid"], clicks)
 639    winter = {"paths": {p: getattr(gt, p) for p in PARTS}, "radii": radii}
 640    # Winter twice: every reading (the truth), and only the ridges, as summer (a check on the ground's depth).
 641    sources = [("winter", winter, images, False, 1.0), ("winter ridges", winter, images, True, 1.0)]
 642    summer = None
 643    for name, mask in (("hand masks", hand), ("U-Net", unet)):
 644        sk = mask_skeleton(mask) if mask is not None else None
 645        if sk is not None:
 646            summer = summer or load_tree(rec, "summer")
 647            sources.append((name, sk, summer, True, SUMMER_SCALE))
 648    rows = []
 649    for name, sk, im, ridge, zoom in sources:
 650        for setting, camera in (SETTINGS.items() if name == "winter" else [("main", SETTINGS["main"])]):
 651            base = {"tree": rec.tree_id, "row": rec.row, "source": name, "setting": setting}
 652            try:
 653                prep = prepare(sk["paths"], im["depth"], im["valid"], sk["radii"], camera, ridge=ridge)
 654            except ValueError as e:
 655                rows.append({**base, "model": "bent", "error": str(e)})
 656                continue
 657            fit = fit_bends(prep, params)
 658            sd = posterior_sd(fit)
 659            rows.append({**base, "model": "plane", "error": "",
 660                         **bend_measures(prep, np.zeros(len(fit["h"])), None, params, zoom)})
 661            rows.append({**base, "model": "bent", "error": "",
 662                         **bend_measures(prep, fit["h"], sd, params, zoom, sigma=fit["sigma"])})
 663            if name == "winter" and setting == "main":
 664                cv = block_cv(prep, grid=[(params.spread, params.length)], base=params, buffer_px=3 * BUFFER_PX)
 665                for key, model in (("plane", "plane"), (setting_key(params), "bent")):
 666                    acc = cv[key]
 667                    n = sum(acc[p][2] for p in PARTS)
 668                    rows[-2 if model == "plane" else -1]["cv_wide_mae"] = (
 669                        sum(acc[p][0] for p in PARTS) / n * 100 if n else float("nan"))
 670    return rows

 688def pooled_mae(cv_rows: Sequence[dict], trees: set | None = None, parts: Sequence[str] = PARTS) -> dict[str, float]:
 689    """Each setting's mean absolute error over all left-out readings of ``trees`` (all if None), in cm."""
 690    tot: dict[str, list[float]] = {}
 691    for r in cv_rows:
 692        if (trees is None or r["tree"] in trees) and r["part"] in parts:
 693            a = tot.setdefault(r["setting"], [0.0, 0])
 694            a[0] += r["sum_abs"]
 695            a[1] += r["n"]
 696    return {k: a[0] / a[1] * 100 for k, a in tot.items() if a[1]}

 699def tree_mae(cv_rows: Sequence[dict], setting: str) -> dict[str, float]:
 700    """Each tree's mean absolute error (cm) over its left-out readings, for one setting."""

 710def choose(cv_rows: Sequence[dict], trees: set | None = None) -> str:
 711    """The rule fixed before the 449-tree run: the prior on :data:`GRID` with the smallest pooled mean
 712    absolute error over the left-out blocks of ``trees`` (all if None)."""

 725def out_of_fold(cv_rows: Sequence[dict], fold_rows: Sequence[Sequence[int]]) -> dict:
 726    """The bends against the plane with the prior chosen on the other folds' trees (row-grouped).
 727
 728    Returns:
 729        ``{"chosen": [key per fold scored], "trees": {tree: (plane MAE, bent MAE)}}`` (cm); a fold with no
 730        trees, or with no trees outside it, is skipped.
 731    """
 732    row_of = {r["tree"]: r["row"] for r in cv_rows}
 733    plane = tree_mae(cv_rows, "plane")
 734    out = {"chosen": [], "trees": {}}
 735    for rows in fold_rows:
 736        inside = {t for t, r in row_of.items() if r in rows}
 737        if not inside or not set(row_of) - inside:          # nothing to score, or nothing to choose on
 738            continue
 739        key = choose(cv_rows, set(row_of) - inside)
 740        out["chosen"].append(key)
 741        bent = tree_mae([r for r in cv_rows if r["tree"] in inside], key)
 742        for t in inside:
 743            if t in bent and t in plane:
 744                out["trees"][t] = (plane[t], bent[t])
 745    return out

 748def _q(values) -> dict:
 749    v = np.asarray([x for x in values if x is not None and np.isfinite(x)], float)
 750    if not len(v):
 751        return {"n": 0, "median": None, "q1": None, "q3": None}
 752    q1, med, q3 = np.percentile(v, [25, 50, 75])
 753    return {"n": int(len(v)), "median": float(med), "q1": float(q1), "q3": float(q3)}

 756def _paired_better(a: dict, b: dict) -> dict:
 757    """Trees in both: how often ``b``'s value is below ``a``'s, the Wilcoxon p, and both medians."""
 758    from scipy import stats
 759
 760    t = [k for k in a if k in b and np.isfinite(a[k]) and np.isfinite(b[k])]
 761    if len(t) < 3:
 762        return {"n": len(t)}
 763    x, y = np.array([a[k] for k in t]), np.array([b[k] for k in t])
 764    d = x - y
 765    return {"n": len(t), "better": float(np.mean(d > 0)), "median_a": float(np.median(x)),
 766            "median_b": float(np.median(y)), "median_gain": float(np.median(d)),
 767            "wilcoxon_p": float(stats.wilcoxon(d).pvalue) if np.any(d != 0) else 1.0}

 774def summer_against_winter(rows: Sequence[dict], source: str, measure: str) -> dict:
 775    """A summer source's bends and its plane against winter's bends, tree by tree, on one measure.
 776
 777    Returns:
 778        ``{"n", "median_bent", "median_plane", "bent_closer", "wilcoxon_p", "spearman"}``: the median
 779        ``|summer − winter|`` with the summer bends and with the summer plane, the share of trees where the
 780        bends are closer, the Wilcoxon p of the paired differences, and the rank correlation of the summer
 781        bends with winter's.
 782    """
 783    from scipy import stats
 784
 785    def get(src, model):
 786        """One source's and model's ``measure`` per tree (main setting, fitted, finite)."""
 787        return {r["tree"]: r[measure] for r in rows if r["source"] == src and r["setting"] == "main"
 788                and r["model"] == model and not r.get("error") and np.isfinite(r.get(measure, np.nan))}
 789
 790    w, b, p = get("winter", "bent"), get(source, "bent"), get(source, "plane")
 791    t = [k for k in w if k in b and k in p]
 792    if len(t) < 3:
 793        return {"n": len(t)}
 794    w_, b_, p_ = (np.array([m[k] for k in t]) for m in (w, b, p))
 795    eb, ep = np.abs(b_ - w_), np.abs(p_ - w_)
 796    d = ep - eb
 797    varied = np.std(b_) > 0 and np.std(w_) > 0
 798    return {"n": len(t), "median_bent": float(np.median(eb)), "median_plane": float(np.median(ep)),
 799            "bent_closer": float(np.mean(d > 0)),
 800            "wilcoxon_p": float(stats.wilcoxon(d).pvalue) if np.any(d != 0) else 1.0,
 801            "spearman": float(stats.spearmanr(w_, b_)[0]) if varied else None}

 804def summarise(cv_rows: Sequence[dict], rows: Sequence[dict], fold_rows: Sequence[Sequence[int]]) -> dict:
 805    """The report: the cross-validation, winter's bends, their sensitivity, and summer against winter."""
 806    from treeskel.tree3d import SETTINGS
 807
 808    chosen = choose(cv_rows)
 809    oof = out_of_fold(cv_rows, fold_rows)
 810    report = {"n_trees_cv": len({r["tree"] for r in cv_rows}), "grid": pooled_mae(cv_rows), "chosen": chosen,
 811              "parts": {p: {k: pooled_mae(cv_rows, parts=[p]).get(k) for k in ("plane", chosen)} for p in PARTS},
 812              "out_of_fold": {"chosen": oof["chosen"],
 813                              **_paired_better({t: v[0] for t, v in oof["trees"].items()},
 814                                               {t: v[1] for t, v in oof["trees"].items()})}}
 815
 816    def col(source, model, measure, setting="main"):
 817        """One source's, model's and setting's ``measure`` per tree (fitted, finite)."""
 818        return {r["tree"]: r[measure] for r in rows if r["source"] == source and r["setting"] == setting
 819                and r["model"] == model and not r.get("error") and np.isfinite(r.get(measure, np.nan))}
 820
 821    report["wide_buffer"] = _paired_better(col("winter", "plane", "cv_wide_mae"), col("winter", "bent", "cv_wide_mae"))
 822    bent = [r for r in rows if r["source"] == "winter" and r["setting"] == "main" and r["model"] == "bent"
 823            and not r.get("error")]
 824    report["winter"] = {k: _q([r[k] for r in bent]) for k in BEND_MEASURES}
 825    for side in ("left", "right"):

 834        sub = [r for r in rows if r["source"] == "winter" and r["setting"] == setting and r["model"] == "bent"
 835               and not r.get("error")]

 838            "trunk_out": _q([r["trunk_out"] for r in sub])["median"],
 839            "bow_abs": _q([abs(r[f"{s}_bow"]) for r in sub for s in ("left", "right")])["median"],

 841            "v_change": _q([vb_[t] - vp_[t] for t in vb_ if t in vp_])["median"]}

 848        sub = [r for r in rows if r["source"] == source and r["model"] == "bent" and not r.get("error")]

 850            "n_trees": len(sub),
 851            "anchors": {p: _q([r[f"{p}_anchors"] for r in sub])["median"] for p in PARTS},

 853            "against_winter": {m: summer_against_winter(rows, source, m) for m in SUMMER_MEASURES}}

 855    # leave them out).

 859        setting: sorted({r["tree"] for r in rows if r["source"] == "winter" and r["setting"] == setting

def prior_terms( prep: Prepared) -> tuple[scipy.sparse._csc.csc_matrix, scipy.sparse._csc.csc_matrix]: 
295def prior_terms(prep: Prepared) -> tuple[sparse.csc_matrix, sparse.csc_matrix]:
296    """The prior's two parts on the chains' nodes: ``(mass, curvature)``.
297
298    ``hᵀ mass h ≈ ∫ h² ds`` and ``hᵀ curvature h ≈ ∫ (d²h/ds²)² ds``, summed over the three chains, with
299    the second differences taken within each chain (each may kink at the fork).
300    """
301    n = len(prep.z_axis)
302    mass = np.zeros(n)
303    rows, cols, vals = [], [], []
304    r = 0
305    for p in PARTS:
306        c, s = prep.chains[p], prep.s[p]
307        ds = np.maximum(np.diff(s), 1e-4)
308        mass[c] += np.r_[0.0, ds / 2] + np.r_[ds / 2, 0.0]
309        for j in range(1, len(c) - 1):
310            a, b = ds[j - 1], ds[j]
311            w = np.sqrt((a + b) / 2)
312            rows += [r, r, r]
313            cols += [c[j - 1], c[j], c[j + 1]]
314            vals += [w * 2 / (a * (a + b)), -w * 2 / (a * b), w * 2 / (b * (a + b))]
315            r += 1
316    g = sparse.csr_matrix((vals, (rows, cols)), shape=(r, n))
317    return sparse.diags(np.maximum(mass, 1e-6)).tocsc(), (g.T @ g).tocsc()

340    """The bends ``h`` (m behind the plane, along each ray) at every node, fitted robustly.
341
342    Solves ``(Q + W/σ²) h = W d/σ²`` with ``Q`` the prior's :func:`precision`, ``d`` the readings' distance
343    behind the plane and ``W`` each reading's weight (its robust weight times ``cell_px / corr_px``).
344    Huber weights with the noise ``σ`` re-estimated each round, then Tukey's biweight with ``σ`` fixed.
345
346    Args:
347        prep: The tree (:func:`prepare`).
348        params: The prior and the robust fit; ``spread`` 0 gives the plane (``h`` = 0).
349        d: Readings behind the plane to use instead of ``prep.d`` (e.g. against a refitted plane).
350        hold: Nodes whose readings are left out.
351        terms: :func:`prior_terms`, to reuse across fits.
352
353    Returns:
354        ``{"h", "weights", "sigma", "observed", "system"}``: ``system`` is the final ``Q + W/σ²`` (for
355        :func:`posterior_sd`), None for the plane.
356    """
357    d = prep.d if d is None else d
358    obs = np.isfinite(d) & (True if hold is None else ~hold)
359    n = len(d)
360    if params.spread <= 0:
361        return {"h": np.zeros(n), "weights": obs.astype(float), "sigma": float("nan"), "observed": obs,
362                "system": None}
363    q = precision(terms or prior_terms(prep), params)
364    if obs.sum() == 0:                                  # nothing seen: the prior alone, the plane and its spread
365        return {"h": np.zeros(n), "weights": np.zeros(n), "sigma": float("nan"), "observed": obs, "system": q}
366    y = np.where(obs, d, 0.0)
367    cell = prep.cell_px / params.corr_px
368    sigma = max(_robust_scale(d[obs]), params.min_noise)
369    w = obs.astype(float)
370    h = np.zeros(n)
371
372    def solve(weights, s):
373        """The bends for these readings' weights and noise level, and the system solved."""
374        a = (q + sparse.diags(cell * weights / s ** 2)).tocsc()
375        return spsolve(a, cell * weights * y / s ** 2), a
376
377    a = None
378    # Huber: bounded influence, the noise level from the residuals each round.
379    for _ in range(params.iters):
380        h_new, a = solve(w, sigma)
381        r = np.where(obs, d - h_new, 0.0)
382        sigma = max(_robust_scale(r[obs]), params.min_noise)
383        w = np.where(obs, np.minimum(1.0, params.huber * sigma / np.maximum(np.abs(r), 1e-12)), 0.0)
384        done = np.max(np.abs(h_new - h)) < 1e-5
385        h = h_new
386        if done:
387            break

389    for _ in range(params.iters):

391        u = r / (params.tukey * sigma)
392        w = np.where(obs & (np.abs(u) < 1), (1 - u ** 2) ** 2, 0.0)
393        h_new, a = solve(w, sigma)
394        done = np.max(np.abs(h_new - h)) < 1e-5
395        h = h_new
396        if done:
397            break
398    return {"h": h, "weights": w, "sigma": sigma, "observed": obs, "system": a}
