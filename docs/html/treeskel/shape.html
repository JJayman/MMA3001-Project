treeskel.shape

The shape of a tree's trunk and V, measured without aligning the seasons.

Winter-to-summer pixel alignment was abandoned in session 7 (docs/results/align/), so the winter traces cannot label the summer photos pixel by pixel. Angles do not need that alignment. A change of camera distance (scale) or position (shift) leaves every angle in the image unchanged. Roll (rotation about the viewing axis) turns every direction by the same amount, so the angle between two parts of the tree is unchanged too. Pitch and yaw add perspective, which changes angles only slightly (perspective_change()).

Each part is summarised by straight lines fitted by total least squares (line_direction()), in an (x, y) frame with y pointing up:

  • Trunk axis: the top of the trunk, from the fork down to trunk_reach units (all of the trunk if it is shorter). The top is used because that is what the leaders grow from, and because the trunk's base is often out of frame.
  • Leader line: each leader from start to reach units from the fork. Nearer the fork, the traced path is still inside the top of the trunk.
  • Halves: the same stretch of each leader, split in two by length.

The unit is 16.5 px of the winter photo (UNIT_PX, the median trunk radius at the fork), so the leader lines run from 25 to 264 px up each leader. Both choices are the student's (session 10):

  • A fixed stretch, not all of the leader in view. The leaders curve, so a line through everything in view depends on where the photo's top edge cuts them, and the summer photos are framed tighter than the winter ones (framing_change()).
  • Pixels, not each tree's own trunk radius (the unit until the 449-tree check). The trunk radius varies by a factor of 2.2 between trees (5th to 95th percentile), so a stretch in trunk radii covered very different lengths of leader; and in the hand masks it is however wide the trunk was drawn. On curved leaders a stretch 25% longer or shorter moves the angles by 2–5°. A length in pixels varies only with the camera's distance (about ±10%). A summer photo shows the tree larger, by SUMMER_SCALE, so a hand mask is measured over the same length times that scale (zoom).

The measures (shape_measures()), all in degrees:

Measure Meaning Camera roll
v_opening angle between the two leader lines no effect
left_angle, right_angle each leader's line from the trunk axis, positive outwards (away from the other leader) no effect
lean the V's bisector from the trunk axis, positive to the right no effect
left_bend, right_bend the upper half's line from the lower half's, positive outwards: how much a leader curves no effect
trunk_tilt the trunk axis from the image's vertical, positive to the right adds to it
left_tilt, right_tilt each leader's line from the image's vertical, positive to the right adds to it
lean_v the V's bisector from the image's vertical adds to it

They are related: v_opening = left_angle + right_angle = right_tilt − left_tilt, lean = (right_angle − left_angle) / 2 = lean_v − trunk_tilt. The trunk axis matters only for the three trunk-relative measures.

The same measures are taken from the provided hand masks (mask_branches()), whose crop and resize (the photo's centre 480x480 to 256x256) scale both directions alike, so their angles need no conversion. The hand masks were drawn on the summer view: laid onto the winter traces (hand_frame()), they turn by the camera's roll between the seasons.

The run (main()), on the development rows only: each tree's winter trace, the straight lines through its clicks (a check on the trace) and its hand mask are measured; how much a tighter framing would change the measures is recorded; and features of the summer photo are computed with a pretrained network (tree_features()) for treeskel.shape_model. The table and the features are committed in data/shape/, so the models can be fitted without the images::

 125class ShapeParams:
 126    """Where on the trunk and leaders the lines are fitted.
 127
 128    Attributes:
 129        start: Leader lines start this many units from the fork, past the

 157def to_xy(path: np.ndarray) -> np.ndarray:
 158    """``(row, col)`` image points as ``(x, y)`` with ``y`` pointing up.
 159
 160    Args:
 161        path: ``(N, 2)`` ``(row, col)`` points.
 162
 163    Returns:
 164        ``(N, 2)`` float ``(x, y)`` points.
 165
 166    Example:
 167        >>> to_xy(np.array([[10, 3]])).tolist()
 168        [[3.0, -10.0]]
 169    """
 170    p = np.asarray(path, float).reshape(-1, 2)
 171    return np.stack([p[:, 1], -p[:, 0]], 1)

 174def line_direction(points: np.ndarray) -> np.ndarray:
 175    """Unit direction of the total-least-squares line through ``(x, y)`` points.
 176
 177    The direction is the principal axis of the centred points (the first
 178    right singular vector), so rows and columns are treated alike and a
 179    vertical line is no harder than any other. It is oriented from the first
 180    point towards the last.
 181
 182    Args:
 183        points: ``(N, 2)`` ``(x, y)`` points, in order.
 184
 185    Returns:
 186        The ``(2,)`` unit direction.
 187
 188    Example:
 189        >>> np.round(line_direction(np.array([[0.0, 0], [1, 1], [2, 2]])), 4).tolist()
 190        [0.7071, 0.7071]
 191    """
 192    p = np.asarray(points, float)
 193    c = p - p.mean(0)
 194    _, _, vt = np.linalg.svd(c, full_matrices=False)
 195    d = vt[0]
 196    return -d if np.dot(p[-1] - p[0], d) < 0 else d

 199def tilt(direction: np.ndarray) -> float:
 200    """Angle of an ``(x, y)`` direction from straight up, positive to the right (degrees).
 201
 202    Args:
 203        direction: An ``(x, y)`` direction (``y`` up).
 204
 205    Returns:
 206        The angle in degrees, in ``(-180, 180]``.
 207
 208    Example:
 209        >>> round(tilt(np.array([1.0, 1.0])), 6), round(tilt(np.array([-1.0, np.sqrt(3)])), 6)
 210        (45.0, -30.0)
 211    """
 212    return float(np.degrees(np.arctan2(direction[0], direction[1])))

 215def wrap(angle: float) -> float:
 216    """An angle in degrees, wrapped to ``(-180, 180]``.
 217
 218    Args:
 219        angle: An angle in degrees.
 220
 221    Returns:
 222        The same angle in ``(-180, 180]``.
 223    """
 224    a = (angle + 180.0) % 360.0 - 180.0
 225    return 180.0 if a == -180.0 else float(a)

 228def unit_px(fork_radius: float, params: ShapeParams = ShapeParams(), zoom: float = 1.0) -> float:
 229    """The unit of ``params`` in a path's own pixels.
 230
 231    Args:
 232        fork_radius: The tree's trunk radius at the fork, in the path's pixels
 233            (the unit if ``params.unit`` is None).
 234        params: Its ``unit``.
 235        zoom: The path's pixels per pixel of the winter photo (1 for a winter
 236            trace; :data:`MASK_ZOOM` for a hand mask).
 237
 238    Returns:
 239        The unit's length in the path's pixels.
 240
 241    Example:
 242        >>> unit_px(20.0), unit_px(20.0, ShapeParams(unit=None)), round(unit_px(20.0, zoom=2.0), 1)
 243        (16.5, 20.0, 33.0)
 244    """
 245    return float(fork_radius) if params.unit is None else params.unit * zoom

 248def trunk_top(trunk: np.ndarray, unit: float, params: ShapeParams = ShapeParams()) -> np.ndarray | None:
 249    """The ``(row, col)`` points of the trunk's top used for its axis, from the fork down, or None if too short.
 250
 251    Args:
 252        trunk: ``(N, 2)`` ``(row, col)`` path from the root to the fork.
 253        unit: The length unit in the path's pixels (:func:`unit_px`).
 254        params: ``trunk_reach`` and ``min_trunk``.
 255
 256    Returns:
 257        The points, or None if the trunk is shorter than ``min_trunk``.
 258    """
 259    p = np.asarray(trunk)[::-1]                          # from the fork down
 260    if len(p) < 2 or arc_length(p)[-1] < params.min_trunk:
 261        return None
 262    return p[arc_length(p) <= max(params.trunk_reach * unit, params.min_trunk)]

 265def trunk_axis(trunk: np.ndarray, unit: float, params: ShapeParams = ShapeParams()) -> np.ndarray | None:
 266    """Direction of the top of the trunk (:func:`trunk_top`), pointing up towards the fork, or None if too short.
 267
 268    Args:
 269        trunk: ``(N, 2)`` ``(row, col)`` path from the root to the fork.
 270        unit: The length unit in the path's pixels (:func:`unit_px`).
 271        params: ``trunk_reach`` and ``min_trunk``.
 272
 273    Returns:
 274        The ``(2,)`` unit ``(x, y)`` direction, or None.
 275    """
 276    top = trunk_top(trunk, unit, params)
 277    return None if top is None else -line_direction(to_xy(top))     # fitted fork -> down; flip to point up

 280def leader_part(leader: np.ndarray, unit: float, params: ShapeParams = ShapeParams()) -> np.ndarray:
 281    """The ``(row, col)`` points of a leader (fork to tip) used for its line: ``start`` to ``reach`` units.
 282
 283    Args:
 284        leader: ``(N, 2)`` ``(row, col)`` path from the fork to the tip.
 285        unit: The length unit in the path's pixels (:func:`unit_px`).
 286        params: ``start`` and ``reach``.
 287
 288    Returns:
 289        The points within the stretch (empty if the leader stops before it).
 290    """
 291    p = np.asarray(leader)
 292    s = arc_length(p)
 293    return p[(s >= params.start * unit) & (s <= params.reach * unit)]

 296def halves(points: np.ndarray) -> tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray]:
 297    """Split a path into its lower and upper half by length (the middle point is in both).
 298
 299    Args:
 300        points: ``(N, 2)`` points along a path.
 301
 302    Returns:
 303        ``(lower, upper)``.
 304    """
 305    s = arc_length(points)
 306    mid = s[-1] / 2 if len(s) else 0.0
 307    return points[s <= mid], points[s >= mid]

 310def shape_measures(trunk: np.ndarray, left: np.ndarray, right: np.ndarray, fork_radius: float,
 311                   params: ShapeParams = ShapeParams(), zoom: float = 1.0) -> dict[str, float]:
 312    """The shape measures of one tree (see the module docstring).
 313
 314    Args:
 315        trunk: ``(N, 2)`` ``(row, col)`` path from the root to the fork.
 316        left: ``(N, 2)`` path from the fork to the left leader's tip (the tip further left).
 317        right: ``(N, 2)`` path from the fork to the right leader's tip.
 318        fork_radius: The trunk's radius at the fork (px of the path), the
 319            unit only if ``params.unit`` is None.
 320        params: Where the lines are fitted.
 321        zoom: The paths' pixels per pixel of the winter photo
 322            (:data:`MASK_ZOOM` for a hand mask).
 323
 324    Returns:
 325        ``{measure: degrees}`` for every name in :data:`MEASURES`, NaN where
 326        a part is too short to fit (e.g. no trunk axis when the tree splits
 327        at the ground), plus ``fork_radius`` and each leader's whole length
 328        in view in units (``left_length``, ``right_length``); a leader
 329        shorter than ``reach`` is measured over less than the fixed stretch.
 330    """
 331    out = dict.fromkeys(MEASURES, float("nan"))
 332    r = float(fork_radius)
 333    unit = unit_px(r, params, zoom)
 334    tilts, bends, lengths = {}, {}, {}
 335    for side, path in (("left", left), ("right", right)):
 336        part = leader_part(path, unit, params)
 337        lengths[side] = float(arc_length(path)[-1] / unit) if len(path) > 1 else 0.0
 338        if len(part) < params.min_points:
 339            continue
 340        tilts[side] = tilt(line_direction(to_xy(part)))
 341        low, up = halves(part)
 342        if min(len(low), len(up)) >= params.min_points:
 343            turn = wrap(tilt(line_direction(to_xy(up))) - tilt(line_direction(to_xy(low))))
 344            bends[side] = turn if side == "right" else -turn       # outwards is positive
 345    axis = trunk_axis(trunk, unit, params)
 346    if axis is not None:
 347        out["trunk_tilt"] = tilt(axis)
 348    if "left" in tilts:
 349        out["left_tilt"] = tilts["left"]
 350        out["left_bend"] = bends.get("left", float("nan"))
 351        if axis is not None:
 352            out["left_angle"] = wrap(out["trunk_tilt"] - tilts["left"])
 353    if "right" in tilts:
 354        out["right_tilt"] = tilts["right"]
 355        out["right_bend"] = bends.get("right", float("nan"))
 356        if axis is not None:
 357            out["right_angle"] = wrap(tilts["right"] - out["trunk_tilt"])
 358    if len(tilts) == 2:
 359        out["v_opening"] = wrap(tilts["right"] - tilts["left"])
 360        out["lean_v"] = (tilts["left"] + tilts["right"]) / 2
 361        if axis is not None:
 362            out["lean"] = wrap(out["lean_v"] - out["trunk_tilt"])
 363    out.update(fork_radius=r, left_length=lengths["left"], right_length=lengths["right"])
 364    return out

 396def polyline(points: Sequence[Sequence[float]]) -> np.ndarray:
 397    """A connected 8-connected ``(row, col)`` pixel path through the given points in order.
 398
 399    Args:
 400        points: ``(row, col)`` points to join, in order.
 401
 402    Returns:
 403        ``(N, 2)`` int pixel path.
 404    """
 405    pts = [tuple(int(round(v)) for v in p) for p in points]
 406    out = [pts[0]]
 407    for a, b in zip(pts[:-1], pts[1:]):
 408        rr, cc = draw_line(a[0], a[1], b[0], b[1])
 409        out.extend(zip(rr[1:].tolist(), cc[1:].tolist()))
 410    return np.array(out, int).reshape(-1, 2)

 429def mask_branches(mask: np.ndarray) -> dict | None:
 430    """The trunk, the two leaders and the trunk's radius, from a hand-drawn mask.
 431
 432    The mask is thinned to its centre lines and the root, fork and leaders are
 433    picked as for the winter depth (:func:`treeskel.winter_gt.find_main_branches`),
 434    as :func:`treeskel.measure_width.hand_mask_profiles` does. The radius is the
 435    median half-width of the trunk 3–40 mask px below the fork.
 436
 437    Args:
 438        mask: ``(256, 256)`` bool hand mask.
 439
 440    Returns:
 441        ``{"trunk", "left", "right"}`` paths in the mask's own ``(row, col)``
 442        pixels, and ``"fork_radius"`` (mask px), or None if no fork is found.
 443    """
 444    m = ndi.binary_fill_holes(mask)
 445    if not m.any():
 446        return None
 447    sk = skeletonize(m)
 448    lab, n = ndi.label(sk, np.ones((3, 3)))
 449    if n > 1:
 450        sk = lab == (np.argmax(ndi.sum(sk, lab, range(1, n + 1))) + 1)
 451    br = find_main_branches(m, sk)
 452    if br["fork"] is None:
 453        return None
 454    edt = ndi.distance_transform_edt(m)
 455    down = br["trunk"][::-1]
 456    s, w = arc_length(down), edt[down[:, 0], down[:, 1]]
 457    near = (s > 3) & (s < 40)
 458    radius = float(np.median(w[near])) if near.sum() > 3 else float(np.median(w))
 459    return {"trunk": br["trunk"], "left": br["left"], "right": br["right"], "fork_radius": radius}

 466def crop_top(path: np.ndarray, top_row: float) -> np.ndarray:
 467    """A leader (fork to tip) as a photo whose top edge is at ``top_row`` would show it.
 468
 469    The path is kept from its start until it first rises above ``top_row``.
 470
 471    Args:
 472        path: ``(N, 2)`` ``(row, col)`` path from the fork to the tip.
 473        top_row: The image row of the new top edge.
 474
 475    Returns:
 476        The visible part of the path.
 477    """
 478    p = np.asarray(path)
 479    above = np.nonzero(p[:, 0] < top_row)[0]
 480    return p if len(above) == 0 else p[:above[0]]

 483def framing_change(trunk: np.ndarray, left: np.ndarray, right: np.ndarray, fork_radius: float,
 484                   keep: float, params: ShapeParams = ShapeParams(), zoom: float = 1.0) -> dict[str, float]:
 485    """How much each measure changes if the photo's top edge were lower.
 486
 487    The top edge is moved down so that only ``keep`` of the height between
 488    the fork and the image's top (row 0) stays in view, which cuts both
 489    leaders at the same row, as a tighter framing would. With the fixed
 490    stretch (``reach``) nothing changes while the stretch stays in view; with
 491    ``reach = inf`` the lines follow the curved leaders' visible part.
 492
 493    Args:
 494        trunk: ``(N, 2)`` ``(row, col)`` path from the root to the fork.
 495        left: ``(N, 2)`` path from the fork to the left leader's tip.
 496        right: ``(N, 2)`` path from the fork to the right leader's tip.
 497        fork_radius: The trunk's radius at the fork (px).
 498        keep: Share of the height from the fork to the top kept in view.
 499        params: The shape measures' settings.
 500        zoom: The paths' pixels per winter-photo pixel.
 501
 502    Returns:
 503        ``{measure: cropped − full}`` in degrees.
 504    """
 505    fork_row = float(np.asarray(trunk)[-1][0])
 506    cut = fork_row * (1.0 - keep)
 507    full = shape_measures(trunk, left, right, fork_radius, params, zoom)
 508    crop = shape_measures(trunk, crop_top(left, cut), crop_top(right, cut), fork_radius, params, zoom)
 509    return {k: crop[k] - full[k] for k in MEASURES}

 512def transform_path(path: np.ndarray, matrix: np.ndarray) -> np.ndarray:
 513    """Apply a 3x3 ``(x, y)`` image transform to a ``(row, col)`` path; returns float ``(row, col)``.
 514
 515    Args:
 516        path: ``(N, 2)`` ``(row, col)`` points.
 517        matrix: 3x3 transform of ``(x, y)`` points.
 518
 519    Returns:
 520        ``(N, 2)`` float ``(row, col)`` points.
 521    """
 522    p = np.asarray(path, float).reshape(-1, 2)
 523    xy1 = np.concatenate([p[:, ::-1], np.ones((len(p), 1))], 1) @ np.asarray(matrix, float).T
 524    return (xy1[:, :2] / xy1[:, 2:3])[:, ::-1]

 527def perspective_change(trunk: np.ndarray, left: np.ndarray, right: np.ndarray, fork_radius: float,
 528                       pitch: float = 0.0, yaw: float = 0.0, roll: float = 0.0, scale: float = 1.0,
 529                       params: ShapeParams = ShapeParams()) -> dict[str, float]:
 530    """How much each measure changes if the camera were turned, as between the seasons.
 531
 532    The paths are moved by the pinhole-camera rotation of
 533    :class:`treeskel.summer_gt.Overlay` (``K R K⁻¹``, with the D435-like focal
 534    length used there) and measured again.
 535
 536    Args:
 537        trunk: ``(N, 2)`` ``(row, col)`` path from the root to the fork.
 538        left: ``(N, 2)`` path from the fork to the left leader's tip.
 539        right: ``(N, 2)`` path from the fork to the right leader's tip.
 540        fork_radius: The trunk's radius at the fork (px).
 541        pitch: Camera tilt about the horizontal axis (degrees).
 542        yaw: Camera turn about the vertical axis (degrees).
 543        roll: Camera roll (degrees, clockwise).
 544        scale: Change of size.
 545        params: The shape measures' settings.
 546
 547    Returns:
 548        ``{measure: turned − original}`` in degrees.
 549    """
 550    from treeskel.summer_gt import Overlay
 551
 552    m = Overlay(pitch=pitch, yaw=yaw, roll=roll, scale=scale).matrix()
 553    full = shape_measures(trunk, left, right, fork_radius, params)
 554    moved = shape_measures(*(transform_path(p, m) for p in (trunk, left, right)), fork_radius * scale, params,
 555                           zoom=scale)                    # a closer camera shows the same stretch larger
 556    return {k: wrap(moved[k] - full[k]) for k in MEASURES}

 559def similarity_fit(src: np.ndarray, dst: np.ndarray, search: int = 120, step: int = 8, iters: int = 50,
 560                   trim: float = 0.8) -> tuple[np.ndarray, float]:
 561    """The rotation, scale and shift that best lay one set of centre-line points onto another.
 562
 563    Iterative closest points: the best whole-pixel shift on a coarse grid
 564    first, then, repeatedly, each ``src`` point is paired with its nearest
 565    ``dst`` point and a similarity is fitted by least squares
 566    (:func:`treeskel.align.fit_transform`) to the closest ``trim`` of the
 567    pairs, so the parts one set shows and the other does not are left out.
 568    Used to find the frame a hand mask was drawn in: a V's lines through its
 569    fork do not pin down the scale well, but they do pin down the rotation.
 570
 571    Args:
 572        src: ``(N, 2)`` ``(x, y)`` points: the set that shows less (every one of its points has a partner).
 573        dst: ``(M, 2)`` ``(x, y)`` points.
 574        search: Largest shift tried first (px), from ``-search`` to ``search``.
 575        step: Spacing of those shifts (px).
 576        iters: Rounds of pairing and fitting.
 577        trim: Fraction of the pairs used in each fit.
 578
 579    Returns:
 580        ``(matrix, residual)``: the 3x3 ``src`` to ``dst`` transform and the
 581        median distance (px) from the moved ``src`` points to ``dst``.
 582    """
 583    from scipy.spatial import cKDTree
 584
 585    from treeskel.align import apply_transform, fit_transform
 586
 587    src, dst = np.asarray(src, float), np.asarray(dst, float)
 588    tree = cKDTree(dst)
 589    shifts = np.arange(-search, search + 1, step)
 590    best = min((np.median(tree.query(src + [dx, dy])[0]), dx, dy) for dx in shifts for dy in shifts)
 591    m = np.array([[1.0, 0, best[1]], [0, 1, best[2]], [0, 0, 1]])
 592    for _ in range(iters):
 593        dist, idx = tree.query(apply_transform(m, src))
 594        keep = dist <= np.quantile(dist, trim)
 595        fit = fit_transform(src[keep], dst[idx[keep]], "similarity")
 596        if fit is None:
 597            break
 598        m = fit
 599    return m, float(np.median(tree.query(apply_transform(m, src))[0]))

 602def hand_frame(winter: dict, hand: dict) -> dict[str, float]:
 603    """The frame a hand mask was drawn in, relative to the winter photo.
 604
 605    Args:
 606        winter: ``{"trunk", "left", "right"}`` traced paths in the winter photo.
 607        hand: The same from :func:`mask_branches` (mask pixels).
 608
 609    Returns:
 610        ``{"rotation", "scale", "residual"}`` of the winter-to-hand-mask
 611        similarity: rotation in degrees, positive clockwise in the photo (as
 612        :func:`treeskel.align.describe`), scale, and the median distance (px)
 613        left between the centre lines. If the masks were drawn on the summer
 614        photos, the rotation is the camera's roll between the seasons. The
 615        scale is hardly determined (lines through the fork map onto
 616        themselves under any scaling about it) and should not be relied on.
 617    """
 618    from treeskel.align import describe
 619
 620    w = np.concatenate([to_xy(winter[k]) for k in ("trunk", "left", "right")]) * [1, -1]      # (x, y down)
 621    h = np.concatenate([mask_to_image(hand[k])[:, ::-1] for k in ("trunk", "left", "right")])
 622    m, residual = similarity_fit(h, w)                     # the mask shows less: lay it on the winter trace
 623    d = describe(np.linalg.inv(m), w.mean(0))              # the inverse: winter to hand mask
 624    return {"rotation": d["rotation"], "scale": d["scale"], "residual": residual}

 631def _segment(points: np.ndarray) -> tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray]:
 632    """End points ``(row, col)`` of a line fitted to ``(row, col)`` points, spanning their extent."""
 633    xy = to_xy(points)
 634    d, c = line_direction(xy), xy.mean(0)
 635    t = (xy - c) @ d
 636    a, b = c + t.min() * d, c + t.max() * d
 637    return np.array([-a[1], a[0]]), np.array([-b[1], b[0]])

 640def fitted_lines(trunk: np.ndarray, left: np.ndarray, right: np.ndarray, fork_radius: float,
 641                 params: ShapeParams = ShapeParams(), zoom: float = 1.0) -> dict[str, tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray]]:
 642    """The lines :func:`shape_measures` fits, as ``(start, end)`` ``(row, col)`` points, for drawing.
 643
 644    Args:
 645        trunk: ``(N, 2)`` ``(row, col)`` path from the root to the fork.
 646        left: ``(N, 2)`` path from the fork to the left leader's tip.
 647        right: ``(N, 2)`` path from the fork to the right leader's tip.
 648        fork_radius: The trunk's radius at the fork (px).
 649        params: The shape measures' settings.
 650        zoom: The paths' pixels per winter-photo pixel.
 651
 652    Returns:
 653        Some of ``"trunk"``, ``"left"``, ``"right"``, ``"left_lower"``, ``"left_upper"``,
 654        ``"right_lower"`` and ``"right_upper"`` (a part too short to fit is left out).
 655    """
 656    out = {}
 657    unit = unit_px(fork_radius, params, zoom)
 658    top = trunk_top(trunk, unit, params)
 659    if top is not None and len(top) >= 2:
 660        out["trunk"] = _segment(top)
 661    for side, path in (("left", left), ("right", right)):
 662        part = leader_part(path, unit, params)
 663        if len(part) < params.min_points:
 664            continue
 665        out[side] = _segment(part)
 666        low, up = halves(part)
 667        if min(len(low), len(up)) >= params.min_points:
 668            out[f"{side}_lower"], out[f"{side}_upper"] = _segment(low), _segment(up)
 669    return out

 730FEATURE_SIZE = (240, 320)
 731"""``(height, width)`` the summer images are resized to for the network: half size, near the
 732224-pixel images ResNet-18 was trained on, so that one layer-4 cell sees a larger part of the tree."""
 733
 734GRID = 2
 735"""The layer-4 features are also averaged over a ``GRID`` x ``GRID`` grid of cells, so that the left
 736and right (and upper and lower) parts of the photo keep their own features."""
 737
 738IMAGENET_MEAN = np.array([0.485, 0.456, 0.406], np.float32)
 739IMAGENET_STD = np.array([0.229, 0.224, 0.225], np.float32)

 742def network_input(image: np.ndarray, size: tuple[int, int] = FEATURE_SIZE, mirror: bool = False) -> np.ndarray:
 743    """An 8-bit colour or depth image as the network's input.
 744
 745    A depth image (one channel) is repeated in all three channels. The image
 746    is flipped left to right first if ``mirror``, resized (bilinear), scaled
 747    to [0, 1] and normalised with the ImageNet mean and standard deviation.
 748
 749    Args:
 750        image: ``(H, W, 3)`` or ``(H, W)`` uint8 image.
 751        size: Output ``(height, width)``.
 752        mirror: Flip left to right first.
 753
 754    Returns:
 755        ``(3, h, w)`` float32 array.
 756
 757    Example:
 758        >>> network_input(np.zeros((480, 640), np.uint8), (24, 32)).shape
 759        (3, 24, 32)
 760    """
 761    from PIL import Image
 762
 763    a = np.asarray(image, np.uint8)
 764    if a.ndim == 2:
 765        a = np.repeat(a[..., None], 3, axis=2)
 766    if mirror:
 767        a = a[:, ::-1]
 768    x = np.asarray(Image.fromarray(np.ascontiguousarray(a)).resize(size[::-1], Image.BILINEAR), np.float32) / 255.0
 769    return ((x - IMAGENET_MEAN) / IMAGENET_STD).transpose(2, 0, 1).copy()

 772def make_resnet18(pretrained: bool = True, device: str | None = None):
 773    """ResNet-18 up to its last convolutional block (layer 4), for extracting features.
 774
 775    Args:
 776        pretrained: Load torchvision's ImageNet weights (downloaded once, about
 777            45 MB, into the torch cache). False gives random weights, for tests.
 778        device: ``"cuda"``, ``"cpu"``, or None for the GPU if there is one.
 779
 780    Returns:
 781        ``(network, device)``; the network is in evaluation mode.
 782    """
 783    import torch
 784    from torchvision.models import ResNet18_Weights, resnet18
 785
 786    device = device or ("cuda" if torch.cuda.is_available() else "cpu")
 787    net = resnet18(weights=ResNet18_Weights.IMAGENET1K_V1 if pretrained else None)
 788    body = torch.nn.Sequential(*list(net.children())[:-2])          # drop the pooling and the classifier
 789    return body.eval().to(device), device

 792def cnn_features(body, device: str, inputs: np.ndarray, grid: int = GRID,
 793                 batch: int = 16) -> tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray]:
 794    """Layer-4 features averaged over the whole map and over a grid of cells.
 795
 796    Args:
 797        body: Network from :func:`make_resnet18`.
 798        device: Its device.
 799        inputs: ``(N, 3, h, w)`` float32 images from :func:`network_input`.
 800        grid: Cells per side.
 801        batch: Images per forward pass.
 802
 803    Returns:
 804        ``(whole, cells)``: ``(N, C)`` and ``(N, grid * grid, C)``, cells in
 805        reading order (top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right for 2).
 806    """
 807    import torch
 808    import torch.nn.functional as F
 809
 810    whole, cells = [], []
 811    with torch.inference_mode():
 812        for i in range(0, len(inputs), batch):
 813            f = body(torch.from_numpy(np.ascontiguousarray(inputs[i:i + batch])).to(device))
 814            whole.append(F.adaptive_avg_pool2d(f, 1).flatten(1).float().cpu().numpy())
 815            cells.append(F.adaptive_avg_pool2d(f, grid).flatten(2).transpose(1, 2).float().cpu().numpy())
 816    return np.concatenate(whole), np.concatenate(cells)

 819def tree_features(body, device: str, summer: dict, size: tuple[int, int] = FEATURE_SIZE) -> dict[str, np.ndarray]:

 822    Args:
 823        body: The network, from :func:`make_resnet18`.
 824        device: Its device, from :func:`make_resnet18`.

 959def save_traces(path: str | Path, ids: Sequence[str], traces: Sequence[dict]) -> None:
 960    """Write the winter traces of many trees to one ``.npz``: each part's points end to end, with offsets.
 961
 962    The traces are the project's own ground truth (clicks traced on the winter photos), a few kB per
 963    tree; with them, other shape measures can be tried without the photos.
 964
 965    Args:
 966        path: The ``.npz`` file.
 967        ids: Tree ids, one per trace.
 968        traces: Per tree, ``{"trunk", "left", "right", "fork_radius"}``.
 969    """
 970    arrays = {"ids": np.array(ids), "fork_radius": np.array([t["fork_radius"] for t in traces], np.float32)}
 971    for part in ("trunk", "left", "right"):
 972        paths = [np.asarray(t[part], np.int16).reshape(-1, 2) for t in traces]
 973        arrays[f"{part}_points"] = np.concatenate(paths)
 974        arrays[f"{part}_offsets"] = np.concatenate([[0], np.cumsum([len(q) for q in paths])]).astype(np.int32)
 975    np.savez_compressed(path, **arrays)

 979    """Inverse of :func:`save_traces`: ``{tree: {"trunk", "left", "right", "fork_radius"}}``.

 981    Args:
 982        path: A file from :func:`save_traces`.

 984    Returns:

 988        out = {}

1001def summarise(rows: Sequence[dict], params: ShapeParams = ShapeParams()) -> dict:
1002    """Per measure: the winter spread, the clicks' and the hand masks' agreement with the winter trace.
1003
1004    Args:
1005        rows: One row per tree (the shape table).
1006        params: The shape measures' settings.
1007
1008    Returns:
1009        ``{"n_trees", "measures": {measure: {...}}, "short_leaders": {...},
1010        "no_trunk_axis": [...], "hand_missing": [...]}``.
1011    """
1012    def col(k):
1013        """One column of the rows as a float array."""
1014        return np.array([r[k] for r in rows], float)
1015
1016    # Each measure's spread over the trees, and how far the clicks alone and the hand masks are from winter.
1017    out = {"n_trees": len(rows), "measures": {}}
1018    for k in MEASURES:
1019        w, c, h = col(f"winter_{k}"), col(f"clicks_{k}"), col(f"hand_{k}")
1020        mean, sd, n = _stats(w)
1021        dc, dh = c - w, h - w
1022        ok = np.isfinite(w) & np.isfinite(h)
1023        out["measures"][k] = {
1024            "winter_mean": mean, "winter_sd": sd, "n": n,
1025            "clicks_median_abs_diff": float(np.nanmedian(np.abs(dc))) if np.isfinite(dc).any() else None,
1026            "hand_mean_diff": float(np.nanmean(dh)) if ok.any() else None,
1027            "hand_median_abs_diff": float(np.nanmedian(np.abs(dh))) if ok.any() else None,
1028            "hand_r": float(np.corrcoef(w[ok], h[ok])[0, 1]) if ok.sum() > 2 else None,
1029            "hand_n": int(ok.sum()),
1030        }
1031    # Leaders shorter than the stretch the angles are fitted over.
1032    short = {}
1033    for src in ("winter", "hand"):
1034        lengths = np.concatenate([col(f"{src}_left_length"), col(f"{src}_right_length")])
1035        lengths = lengths[np.isfinite(lengths)]
1036        short[src] = {"fraction": float(np.mean(lengths < params.reach)) if len(lengths) else None,
1037                      "n_leaders": int(len(lengths)),
1038                      "median_length": float(np.median(lengths)) if len(lengths) else None}
1039    out["short_leaders"] = short
1040    if rows and "winter_radii_v_opening" in rows[0]:
1041        # The same agreement with each tree's own trunk radius as the unit (the hand masks' drawn one).
1042        out["radii_unit"] = {}
1043        for k in MEASURES:
1044            w, h = col(f"winter_radii_{k}"), col(f"hand_radii_{k}")
1045            ok = np.isfinite(w) & np.isfinite(h)
1046            out["radii_unit"][k] = {
1047                "winter_sd": _stats(w)[1],
1048                "hand_median_abs_diff": float(np.median(np.abs(h - w)[ok])) if ok.any() else None,
1049                "hand_r": float(np.corrcoef(w[ok], h[ok])[0, 1]) if ok.sum() > 2 else None}
1050    out["no_trunk_axis"] = [r["tree"] for r in rows if not np.isfinite(r["winter_trunk_tilt"])]
1051    out["hand_missing"] = [r["tree"] for r in rows if not np.isfinite(r["hand_v_opening"])]
1052    # How much a tighter framing moves the measures, with the fixed stretch and with the whole leader.
1053    framing = {}
1054    for label in ("fixed", "whole"):
1055        for keep in FRAMING_KEEP:
1056            for k in FRAMING_MEASURES:
1057                name = f"framing_{label}_{round(keep * 100)}_{k}"
1058                v = np.abs(col(name)) if rows and name in rows[0] else np.array([])
1059                v = v[np.isfinite(v)]
1060                framing[name[len("framing_"):]] = ({"median": float(np.median(v)), "p95": float(np.percentile(v, 95)),
1061                                                    "over_1deg": float(np.mean(v > 1.0)), "n": int(len(v))}
1062                                                   if len(v) else None)
1063    out["framing_change"] = framing
1064    if rows and "hand_frame_rotation" in rows[0]:
1065        # The hand masks laid onto the winter traces: the rotation is the camera's roll between the seasons,
1066        # summarised over the well-fitting trees, overall and per orchard row.
1067        rot, res, scale = col("hand_frame_rotation"), col("hand_frame_residual"), col("hand_frame_scale")
1068        good = np.isfinite(rot) & (res <= GOOD_FIT_PX)
1069        by_row = {}
1070        for orchard_row in sorted({r["row"] for r in rows}):
1071            sel = good & (col("row") == orchard_row)
1072            if sel.any():
1073                by_row[int(orchard_row)] = {"median": float(np.median(rot[sel])), "n": int(sel.sum())}
1074        out["hand_frame"] = {
1075            "n": int(np.isfinite(rot).sum()), "good_fits": int(good.sum()), "good_fit_px": GOOD_FIT_PX,
1076            "residual_median": float(np.nanmedian(res)) if np.isfinite(res).any() else None,
1077            "rotation_mean": float(rot[good].mean()) if good.any() else None,
1078            "rotation_sd": float(rot[good].std(ddof=1)) if good.sum() > 1 else None,
1079            "scale_median": float(np.median(scale[good])) if good.any() else None,
1080            "rotation_by_row": by_row,
1081        }
1082    return out

317        left: ``(N, 2)`` path from the fork to the left leader's tip (the tip further left).
318        right: ``(N, 2)`` path from the fork to the right leader's tip.
319        fork_radius: The trunk's radius at the fork (px of the path), the
320            unit only if ``params.unit`` is None.

322        zoom: The paths' pixels per pixel of the winter photo

329        in view in units (``left_length``, ``right_length``); a leader
330        shorter than ``reach`` is measured over less than the fixed stretch.
331    """

333    r = float(fork_radius)
334    unit = unit_px(r, params, zoom)
335    tilts, bends, lengths = {}, {}, {}
336    for side, path in (("left", left), ("right", right)):
337        part = leader_part(path, unit, params)

342        low, up = halves(part)
343        if min(len(low), len(up)) >= params.min_points:

346    axis = trunk_axis(trunk, unit, params)
347    if axis is not None:
348        out["trunk_tilt"] = tilt(axis)
349    if "left" in tilts:
350        out["left_tilt"] = tilts["left"]
351        out["left_bend"] = bends.get("left", float("nan"))
352        if axis is not None:
353            out["left_angle"] = wrap(out["trunk_tilt"] - tilts["left"])
354    if "right" in tilts:

356        out["right_bend"] = bends.get("right", float("nan"))
357        if axis is not None:

362        if axis is not None:
363            out["lean"] = wrap(out["lean_v"] - out["trunk_tilt"])

def similarity_fit( src: numpy.ndarray, dst: numpy.ndarray, search: int = 120, step: int = 8, iters: int = 50, trim: float = 0.8) -> tuple[numpy.ndarray, float]: 
560def similarity_fit(src: np.ndarray, dst: np.ndarray, search: int = 120, step: int = 8, iters: int = 50,
561                   trim: float = 0.8) -> tuple[np.ndarray, float]:
562    """The rotation, scale and shift that best lay one set of centre-line points onto another.
563
564    Iterative closest points: the best whole-pixel shift on a coarse grid
565    first, then, repeatedly, each ``src`` point is paired with its nearest
566    ``dst`` point and a similarity is fitted by least squares
567    (:func:`treeskel.align.fit_transform`) to the closest ``trim`` of the
568    pairs, so the parts one set shows and the other does not are left out.
569    Used to find the frame a hand mask was drawn in: a V's lines through its
570    fork do not pin down the scale well, but they do pin down the rotation.
571
572    Args:
573        src: ``(N, 2)`` ``(x, y)`` points: the set that shows less (every one of its points has a partner).
574        dst: ``(M, 2)`` ``(x, y)`` points.
575        search: Largest shift tried first (px), from ``-search`` to ``search``.
576        step: Spacing of those shifts (px).
577        iters: Rounds of pairing and fitting.
578        trim: Fraction of the pairs used in each fit.
579
580    Returns:
581        ``(matrix, residual)``: the 3x3 ``src`` to ``dst`` transform and the
582        median distance (px) from the moved ``src`` points to ``dst``.
583    """
584    from scipy.spatial import cKDTree
585
586    from treeskel.align import apply_transform, fit_transform
587
588    src, dst = np.asarray(src, float), np.asarray(dst, float)
589    tree = cKDTree(dst)
590    shifts = np.arange(-search, search + 1, step)
591    best = min((np.median(tree.query(src + [dx, dy])[0]), dx, dy) for dx in shifts for dy in shifts)
592    m = np.array([[1.0, 0, best[1]], [0, 1, best[2]], [0, 0, 1]])
593    for _ in range(iters):
594        dist, idx = tree.query(apply_transform(m, src))
595        keep = dist <= np.quantile(dist, trim)
596        fit = fit_transform(src[keep], dst[idx[keep]], "similarity")
597        if fit is None:
598            break
599        m = fit
600    return m, float(np.median(tree.query(apply_transform(m, src))[0]))
