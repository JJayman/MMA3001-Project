# Session summary: 27 September 2026 (session 10)

Learning the tree's shape from winter and predicting it from summer, without
aligning the seasons (the student's first item since session 7).

- **The measures.** The winter traces became angles: the V opening, each
  leader's angle and bend. On the way, the unit of the measures changed from
  each tree's trunk radius to pixels.
- **The provided hand masks,** a person's reading of the summer photos,
  predict the shape well.
- **Fixed ImageNet features** with ridge regression barely beat the mean
  shape.
- **Fine-tuning ResNet-18** does better, mainly on the trunk. The summer depth
  adds nothing, and the full-size photo helps a little.

Continues from [session 9](2026-09-27-session-9.md). Details and all numbers
are in `docs/results/shape/README.md`.

## 1. The shape measures (`treeskel.shape`)

- **The lines:** straight lines fitted by total least squares to the top of
  the trunk (66 px below the fork) and to a fixed stretch of each leader,
  25–264 px from the fork in the winter photo and ×1.12 in the summer view.
- **The measures:** the V opening; each leader's angle from the trunk and
  from the vertical; the V's lean; each leader's bend (its upper half against
  its lower half).
- **Checks with known answers:**
  - synthetic V trees are measured to within 0.15–0.5°;
  - camera roll leaves the roll-free measures unchanged;
  - 5–10° of pitch or yaw moves them by less than 0.8°;
  - a mirror image gives the mirrored measures.
- The same measures are taken from the clicks alone (a check on the traces)
  and from the hand masks (the baseline).

## 2. Choices and their evidence (449 development trees)

| Choice (the student's) | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Both references for the leader angles; the V opening and the bends, which need none, as the headline | The trunk's lean varies (SD 15.0°) but the trellis keeps the V's bisector near vertical (SD 6.0°), so angles from the trunk mostly measure the trunk. Angles from the vertical carry the camera's roll between the seasons. |
| A fixed stretch of each leader, not all of it in view | The leaders curve: a 15% tighter framing moves the V opening by a median 3.1° with the whole leader, 0.0° with the fixed stretch |
| The stretch in pixels, not in trunk radii (after the outlier check, section 4) | The winter trunk radius varies ×2.2 between trees, and a hand mask's trunk is drawn 0.73–3 times as wide. A stretch 20–25% longer or shorter moves the angles by 2–4.5° (median). With pixels, the hand masks correlate better with winter on every measure (bends r 0.72/0.67 → 0.86/0.80). |

## 3. The hand masks are in the summer view

- **They have the winter shape.** Laid onto their winter traces by rotation,
  scale and shift, all 448 fit within 8 px (median 2.2 px).
- **Their rotation is the camera's roll.** It follows the session-7 pairs'
  rotation tree by tree (r = +0.73, 11 trees). It differs by row, with medians
  from −3.6° to +2.0°, which caps what any model can reach on the angles from
  the vertical.
- They agree with the winter traces closely: V opening r = 0.93, median
  difference 2.6°.

## 4. The outlier check (the student's)

The 14 trees where the hand masks and the winter traces disagreed most were
drawn in both seasons (`--trees`).

- **Winter traces:** every one was right (13 checked by the student, R09N32 by
  the AI).
- **Hand masks:** slightly off the branch, shifted sideways, or seen from a
  different view.
- **The measure's unit:** the hand masks' drawn trunk widths had set their
  stretch up to three times too long, which led to the switch to pixels.

## 5. Predicting the shape from the summer photo

(MAE / skill over the mean shape, 5 row-grouped folds, 449 trees)

| Measure | Mean shape | Ridge, fixed ResNet-18 features | CNN fine-tuned, 240x320 | + depth | 480x640 | Hand masks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V opening | 9.8° | 9.2° / +0.10 | 8.9° / +0.16 | 8.8° / +0.15 | 8.7° / +0.18 | 3.3° / +0.87 |
| Left / right bend | 9.5° / 8.8° | +0.03 / −0.01 | +0.07 / +0.05 | +0.07 / −0.04 | +0.06 / +0.05 | +0.70 / +0.61 |
| Trunk from vertical | 10.9° | +0.03 | +0.30 | +0.28 | +0.38 | +0.70 |
| V's lean from trunk | 12.3° | 0.00 | +0.22 | +0.22 | +0.32 | +0.73 |
| Left / right leader from vertical | 7.1° / 6.9° | +0.09 / +0.05 | +0.18 / +0.13 | +0.18 / +0.12 | +0.24 / +0.15 | +0.80 / +0.73 |

- **Fixed features:** ridge regression, and k-nearest neighbours and kernel
  ridge on the same features, all stay at +0.11 or less. The features barely
  tell a photo from its mirror image (r = 0.95).
- **Fine-tuning:**
  - ResNet-18 with a head for all ten measures, the loss skipping unknown
    measures, and mirror images with the mirrored targets.
  - The number of epochs is chosen on an inner fold.
  - 6 minutes on the student's GPU (19 at 480x640).
  - It beats the fixed features on every measure, and the mean shape
    significantly on eight of ten.
  - Its training loss falls to 0.12 while the held-out loss stays at 0.7–0.8.
- **The variants, tree by tree against the first run:**
  - depth: no gain; the right bend slightly worse (p = 0.008);
  - 480x640: better on three measures (p < 0.01); the trunk's tilt higher in
    all 5 folds; the V opening and the bends unchanged.
  - All runs are reported rather than the best one picked on the same folds.

## 6. Decisions (the student's)

- Both angle references, with the V opening and the bends as the headline.
- A fixed stretch of each leader; after the outlier check, its length in
  pixels.
- Derived features and traces committed (`data/shape/`), so the models run
  anywhere.
- Models tried: pretrained CNN features with ridge, then fine-tuning, then
  depth and full size.
- Check the outlier trees before choosing a model.
- The hand masks stay validation only for now. Training on them is the next
  step to consider.

## 8. AI use in this session (for the reflection)

- **What was mine:**
  - every choice in section 6;
  - five runs on my PC (the measures twice, three fine-tunings);
  - installing torchvision;
  - checking 13 outlier trees by eye and giving the verdicts that found no
    tracing errors and led to the unit change;
  - asking whether training on the hand masks would actually help before
    deciding.
- **AI errors, and how they were caught:**
  - **The unit of the stretch.** The AI recommended trunk radii after checking
    framing but not how reliable the unit was. My outlier check led it to find
    the ×2.2 spread and the drawn trunks; the hand-mask baseline had been
    understated (V opening +0.74 → +0.87).
  - **A reading from 11 trees:** a systematic +3° roll between the seasons. On
    449 it was row 1's value.
  - **The README said the PyTorch install includes torchvision.** Mine did
    not, and the run stopped.
  - **A partial test run** (`--sets rgb`) wrote over the saved report; it was
    caught and the full run redone.
  - **NaN handling:** mirroring targets by matrix multiplication would have
    spread one unknown measure to all ten (NaN × 0). Caught while writing the
    CNN code, before any run.
  - **Wrong test expectations,** about seven times: pixel rounding, path
    length, mirror features, a scale a V cannot fix, and run names. The code
    was right each time.
  - **Figures:** labels over whiskers, the legend over the bars, a KeyError;
    fixed after looking at them.
  - **Wording** checked against the saved numbers: the penalty range, a skill
    range, an "every measure" with an exception.
  - **Blocked:** download.pytorch.org (network policy), so PyTorch was tested
    here from PyPI with random weights. A read of the proxy's configuration
    was denied and not pursued.
- **Lessons:**
  - A unit needs the same scrutiny as the thing it measures.
  - My look at the actual trees found what the summary numbers hid, again.
  - With folds that swing widely, compare models tree by tree.
  - Claims from a few trees (here, 11) failed on 449 once more.
