Segmentation trained on the provided hand masks, scored against the winter
traces (the student's first item from session 10).

- **The decision:** the development rows' hand masks became training labels
  (replacing "validation only" from session 2). The winter traces stay the

- **First run, masks as drawn:** worse than the angle CNN (V opening +0.06).

## 1. The segmentation (`treeskel.segment`)

- **Network:** U-Net with a ResNet-18 encoder (ImageNet weights; ResNet-34 as
  an option, as Chen et al.). Input: the masks' own frame, the photo's centre
  480x480 at 256 px.
- **Training:** BCE + Dice loss; random crop, ±10° rotation, mirror and colour
  jitter, with the mask resampled alike; the mirror image averaged in at
  prediction.
- **Validation:** the angle CNN's 5 row-grouped folds, with the epochs chosen
  on an inner fold. The recipe was fixed before any result.
- **Scoring:** each predicted mask is measured exactly as the hand masks are
  and scored against the winter traces; tree by tree against the other runs.
  A mask with no fork counts as the mean shape rather than being dropped.
- **Checks with known answers:** the hand masks passed through the scoring
  path reproduce their committed measures (to 0.0005°). A network trained from
  scratch on synthetic V photos reaches Dice 0.96.

## 2. First run: masks as drawn (449 trees, 17.5 min on the student's GPU)

| Measure (skill) | Hand masks | Angle CNN 480x640 | U-Net, as drawn |
|---|---|---|---|
| V opening | +0.87 | +0.18 | +0.06 |
| Trunk from vertical | +0.70 | +0.38 | +0.27 |

- It was worse than the angle CNN on several measures, tree by tree.
- **Diagnosis** (`docs/results/segment/diagnostics.py`):
  - it did not reproduce even the hand masks' own angles (r 0.43; r 0.42 with
    winter), with two thirds of the real spread: it drew a typical V;
  - the best shift of each prediction onto its hand mask raised the median
    Dice from 0.39 to 0.57 (21 local trees).

## 3. The masks are off the branch, and lining them up

- **Found before the first run** (the AI, by eye, 21 local development trees):
  several masks sit 20–60 px from the visible summer trunk, R20N06 about 90.
  Session 3 had seen this on the sample; session 10 had checked only shape,
  which a shift does not change.
- **`treeskel.annotate_mask`** (the student's request after the first run):
  - the session-7 window, reused;
  - drag or arrow keys to shift, Enter to save, s to skip, f to flag;
  - shifts saved in `annotations/mask_overlay.json`.
- **The line-up** (`docs/results/segment/lineup.py`), all 449 development
  masks, 28 minutes:
  - 96% moved more than 3 px, median 26 px, range −81 to +98 px, almost
    entirely sideways, no scaling;
  - row medians −45 to +56 px, but still 23–41 px SD within a row, so no
    per-row correction would do;
  - flagged: R01N01 (looks like a different tree) and R11N10 (no visible
    branch or trunk feature).
- **Repeatability:** 20 random trees redone from no shift differ by a median
  1.0 px across and 2.3 px down (at most 7 px); r = 0.997.
- The five trees the AI had judged clearly off were moved the way and about as
  far as judged.

## 4. Second run: lined-up masks (20.4 min)

| Measure (skill) | Hand masks | Best angle CNN | U-Net, as drawn | **U-Net, lined up** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V opening | +0.87 | +0.18 | +0.06 | **+0.72** |
| Left / right bend | +0.70 / +0.61 | +0.06 / +0.05 | +0.02 / +0.03 | **+0.57 / +0.47** |
| Left / right leader from the trunk | +0.75 / +0.75 | | +0.03 / +0.19 | **+0.61 / +0.67** |
| Trunk from vertical | +0.70 | +0.38 | +0.27 | **+0.62** |

- **Tree by tree:** 61–75% of trees closer than both the first run and the
  best angle CNN, on all ten measures (p < 1e-9). V opening MAE 8.8–9.6° →
  4.5°.
- **It now reads the tree:** V opening r = 0.85 with winter, 0.89 with the hand
  masks; spread 11.1° against 12.4°; the +4.1° bias gone.
- **Pixels:** Dice 0.79 against the lined-up masks. Against the drawn masks
  0.22, and 0.80 after the best shift: its masks sit where the labels were
  moved.
- **The 14 masks with no fork** (without them the V opening is +0.78):
  - all are good masks (Dice 0.52–0.80), broken into pieces, and the
    measuring keeps only the largest;
  - about half are cut by 2–12 px (R01N14, R02N07, R18N18, R07N18);
  - the others are scattered 25–90 px apart (R05N05, R05N06, R14N30,
    R17N07): real failures.
- **Why the line-up mattered so much:** the offsets (median 26 px) exceed a
  leader's width, so many labels lay beside the branch; the network learned a
  blurred typical V. A translation-free score could not show that.

## 7. AI use in this session (for the reflection)

- **What was mine:**
  - the decision to train on the hand masks;
  - both runs on my PC;
  - judging the first run as not improved and choosing to line the masks up;
  - lining up all 449 masks and redoing 20 for repeatability;
  - flagging R01N01 and R11N10;
  - judging that the masks were about as accurate as our winter masks.
- **AI errors, and how they were caught:**
  - **A first contact sheet included four test-row trees** (rows 3 and 12 of
    the sample). The AI noticed before viewing it, deleted it and rebuilt it
    from development trees only.
  - **The AI's first expectation was that lining up would help little,**
    since angles do not depend on position. The gain was large: the offsets
    had spoiled the learning, not the scoring.
  - **"mask_scores.csv is missing"** was wrong: the AI's own file listing
    filtered out names starting with `mask_`. The student checked GitHub.
  - **Figures:** a loop variable overwrote the new title argument, and
    another the legend names (caught by looking at the figure and a crash);
    labels too small after halving the example sheet.
  - **Wrong test expectations** (the code was right): an arithmetic slip, a
    run name that includes the size, too few training steps for a network
    trained from scratch.
- **Lessons:**
  - A check blind to an error cannot clear it: session 10's shape-only check
    could not see that the masks were in the wrong place.
  - Measure the cheap diagnostic before the expensive fix, but let the
    experiment decide; the AI's expectation here was too pessimistic.
  - Keep test-row data out of even quick looks.
